01 / OUR STORY

We wanted a fun, no‑pressure software engineering club…

…where we could build software for Hopkins.

That's when HopBuilds was born.

Hopkins is full of brilliant CS students, but not a lot of great software built for the Hopkins community. We're changing that: rebranding JHU's Software Engineering Club into a student product team that designs and ships tools making student life more connected and convenient.

  1. Direct mentorship. Direct mentorship.

    01: Mentorship & guidance

    You build with mentorship and guidance from Professor Madooei the whole way, someone who teaches this for a living. And you get SWE and big tech recruiting mentorship from upperclassmen and alumni who have already been through the process themselves.

  2. A real team. A real team.

    02: Real experience

    Teams of 3–4, led by an experienced builder. You build every week and demo your progress each weekend.

  3. Shipped work. Shipped work.

    03: Resume

    Products used by 500+ Hopkins students, portfolio pieces that actually stand out on a resume.

  4. A demo day. A demo day.

    04: Showcase

    Every semester closes with a final presentation: your team, your product, in front of the club and beyond.

04 / THE SOCIAL SIDE

It's not all commits.

Hikes on Mount Rainier, late-night poker games, group food runs, and a team that actually likes each other. HopBuilds is not only about shipping code but also about creating lifelong friendships and lasting memories.

05 / OUR SPONSORS
