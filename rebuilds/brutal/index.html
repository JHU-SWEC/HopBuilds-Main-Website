Our story

Hopkins has brilliant CS students but little software made for Hopkins. We're building it.

A We wanted a fun, no-pressure club where we could build software for our own campus. That's how SWEC @ JHU started.

B We turned JHU's Software Engineering Club into a student product team that designs and ships tools to make campus life more connected and convenient.

The semester

How a semester runs

You work on a small team with an experienced lead and ship something real by the end of term.

  1. 1

    Join a team of 6–7

    Each team is led by an experienced builder who has shipped before.

  2. 2

    Build every week

    Your team picks up real work on its product each week of term.

  3. 3

    Demo each weekend

    Show the club what changed that week, and use its feedback to plan the next one.

  4. 4

    Present at Demo Day

    The semester closes with your team presenting its product to the club and beyond.

Roles

Three roles on every team

You don't need to be a coding wizard. We want people who care about technology and design and will put in the work.

Developers

Designers

Strategists

Drive marketing and the product decisions behind each launch.
